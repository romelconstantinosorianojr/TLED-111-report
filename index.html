02 · Definition

What is Medical Transcription?

Medical transcription is the process of listening to audio recordings dictated by healthcare professionals and converting them into accurate written documents.

Examples of written documents

  • Patient histories
  • Diagnostic reports
  • Operative notes
  • Discharge summaries
🎙️📄〰️
“The spoken report becomes a written medical record.”
03 · Scope of Work

Scope of Medical Transcription

📋Healthcare Documentation

Ensures patient records are complete and accessible.

🩺Support for Physicians

Saves time so doctors can focus on patient care.

💻Technology

Works with speech recognition and electronic health records (EHR).

🌐Career Opportunities

Hospitals, clinics, outsourcing companies, and freelance work.

💻🩺📑
“I listen, type, review, and prepare reports.”
04 · Role in Healthcare

Why does it matter?

✓ Accuracy: Critical for diagnosis, treatment, and continuity of care.

✓ Communication: Provides written records that can be shared across healthcare teams.

✓ Legal Evidence: Medical records serve as official documents in legal and insurance cases.

✓ Patient Safety: Clear documentation helps prevent errors involving medications, procedures, and instructions.

🛡️📋✓
“Accurate records help healthcare teams make informed decisions.”
05 · Case Study

Importance of Accurate Records

Scenario

A physician dictates a patient's medication dosage.

🎙️ Physician's intended dosage

15 mg

⌨️ Transcription error

50 mg

Error: The transcriptionist mishears “15 mg” as “50 mg.”

Impact: The patient may receive an incorrect dosage, creating a risk of harm.

🎙️⚠️💊
“One wrong number can change the meaning of a medical record.”
06 · Case Analysis

What should happen next?

Identify the problem

The recorded dosage and written dosage do not match.

Correct action

  • Review the original audio recording.
  • Verify unclear information using the approved procedure.
  • Correct the transcript before finalizing it.
  • Never guess a medication dosage.

Key lesson: Accuracy protects patient safety and treatment outcomes.

🔍✓🛡️
“When something is unclear, verify it instead of guessing.”

08 · Final Slide

Teaching Activity: Listen & Transcribe

Listen to the sample dictation. Type what you hear, then compare your transcript with the sample answer.

🎙️ Classroom dictation

“The patient reports a mild headache since yesterday. The patient was advised to rest and drink enough water. Follow-up is recommended if the symptoms continue.”

Sample transcript

The patient reports a mild headache since yesterday. The patient was advised to rest and drink enough water. Follow-up is recommended if the symptoms continue.

Compare wording, punctuation, and completeness. This is a fictional classroom example, not an actual patient record.
